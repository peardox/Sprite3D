Fields

Public MainLayout: TLayout;

Holds the progress bar, the status line and the list of results.

Public ExportProgressBar: TProgressBar;

How far the whole export has got, from 0 to 100.

Public RemainingLabel: TLabel;

The estimated time left, under the progress bar, as hours, minutes and seconds; hidden by Finish.

Public StatusLabel: TLabel;

What is being exported now, under the progress bar; the outcome at the end.

Public ResultMemo: TMemo;

The files saved and the problems met, as they happen, and the report at the end.

Public ButtonLayout: TLayout;

The strip holding Abort and Close.

Public AbortButton: TButton;

Asks for the export to stop once the animation being exported is saved; also pressed by the Escape key.

Public CloseButton: TButton;

Closes the dialog; enabled once the export has ended.

Public procedure SetProgress(const AFraction: Single; const AStatus: String);

Shows how far the export has got and what it is doing.

Once Abort has been asked for, the status line keeps saying the export is stopping, after AStatus.

Parameters
AFraction
How far the whole export has got, from 0 to 1; values outside are clamped.
AStatus
What is being done now, in a few words.

Public procedure SetRemaining(const AMilliseconds: Int64);

Shows the estimated time left, as Time remaining: about H:MM:SS.

Hours are not wrapped into days, so 30 hours show as 30:00:00. Ignored after Finish.

Parameters
AMilliseconds
The time left, in milliseconds; below 0 shows that it is still being worked out.
Public procedure SetRemainingNote(const ANote: String);

Shows a note in place of the time left, for a stage that cannot be estimated.

Parameters
ANote
The note, after Time remaining:; empty clears the line.
Public procedure Finish(const AReport: String; const AProblems: TArray<String>);

Shows the outcome, as the export has ended, and lets the dialog be closed.

The status line shows AReport; the list gets AReport and then each of AProblems. Abort is disabled and Close enabled, and the time remaining is hidden. The bar is left where the export got to.

Parameters
AReport
What was exported, in a line or two.
AProblems
What went wrong, one entry each; empty if nothing did.
Public procedure ShowAndFree;

Shows the dialog without blocking and frees it when it is closed.

For Android and iOS, where ShowModal cannot block.
