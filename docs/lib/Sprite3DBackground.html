Unit Sprite3DBackground

Description

A background for a Gorilla3D view: none, a checker for transparency, a colour or a gradient.

TSprite3DBackgroundSpec describes a background: its kind, its colours and the size of the checker squares. TSprite3DBackground shows one behind a viewport. It is not part of the 3D scene: it is an ordinary 2D control, a sibling placed directly behind the viewport and covering the same area, and the viewport's Color is made fully transparent, so the background shows wherever the scene draws nothing. This needs a Gorilla3D whose viewport honours the alpha of its Color, as Gorilla3D 1.5.3 does.

Being 2D, the background does not depend on the camera at all: it is the same in perspective and orthographic projection, at any field of view or zoom, and it never appears in off-screen grabs, which draw the scene into a frame buffer of their own. It is painted from a bitmap, kept until the control's size, the display scale or the spec changes. The checker is drawn at the display's own pixel size, one bitmap pixel to one screen pixel, so its edges stay sharp. The gradient is drawn at no more than MaxGradientPixels pixels along its longer side and stretched smoothly to fit, as a smooth blend loses nothing by it. A Solid background needs no bitmap and is filled directly.

The None kind, the default, hides the control and gives the viewport back the opaque Color it had when the background was attached, so the view looks as it did before any background existed. The Transparent kind shows a checker of two greys, the usual sign that an image is transparent there. The squares are CheckerSize logical pixels on screen, counted from the view's top-left corner, which is light.

The same spec is meant for TSprite3DOffscreen.Backdrop, which gives grabs the same background: transparent for None and for Transparent, as the checker stands for, and the colour or gradient otherwise. NewBitmap paints a spec into a bitmap of any size for such uses. 

ViewportLayout (or whatever holds the viewport)
|-- TSprite3DBackground   2D, behind; same area as the viewport
`-- TGorillaViewport      Color fully transparent unless the kind is None

Description

Types

TSprite3DBackgroundKind = (...);

What a background shows.

Values
  • None: No background is drawn: the viewport's own opaque colour shows, and grabs are transparent. The default.
  • Transparent: A checker on screen, standing for transparency; transparency in grabs.
  • Solid: One colour, Colour.
  • Gradient: A blend from GradientFrom to GradientTo, in the direction GradientDirection.
TSprite3DGradientDirection = (...);

Which way a Gradient background runs, from GradientFrom to GradientTo.

The diagonals run corner to corner whatever the view's shape: the colour is constant along lines parallel to the other diagonal, as in CSS's to bottom right.

Values
  • TopToBottom: From the top edge to the bottom edge. The default.
  • LeftToRight: From the left edge to the right edge.
  • TopLeftToBottomRight: From the top-left corner to the bottom-right corner.
  • TopRightToBottomLeft: From the top-right corner to the bottom-left corner.
