Unit

Sprite3DCamera

Declaration

type TSprite3DCamera = class(TGorillaCamera)

Description

A TGorillaCamera that orbits a centre point through a chain of three pivots.

The constructor builds the pivot chain and makes the camera a child of RollPivot. Place the camera in a scene by setting Rig.Parent, never the camera's own Parent. Leave TCamera.Target nil: the pivots aim the camera at OrbitCenter.

The pivots have no owner and are not stored in .fmx files. Freeing the camera frees the pivots; freeing Rig frees the pivots and the camera. Like any FMX object, use it from the main thread only. The class is registered with RegisterFmxClasses.

Public constructor Create(AOwner: TComponent); override;

Creates the camera and its pivot chain.

Distance starts at 10 and all three angles at 0. If AOwner is a TGorillaViewport, Rig is parented to it straight away; otherwise set Rig.Parent before the camera can render anything.

Parameters
AOwner
The owner of the camera itself, not of the pivots. If it is a TGorillaViewport it also becomes the parent of Rig.
Public destructor Destroy; override;

Frees the camera and, if it was freed directly, its pivot chain.

When the camera is freed directly, for example by its owner, it detaches from RollPivot and frees Rig, which frees PolarPivot and RollPivot with it. When Rig is being freed by its own parent, the pivots are already on their way out and are left alone.

Public procedure LookAt(const APoint: TPoint3D; const AMode: TLookAtMode = lmMinimalTwist);

Turns the camera, from where it stands, to face APoint.

The turn is the shortest-arc rotation from the current view direction to the new one. No world-up vector is involved, so it works looking straight up or down and never flips; a 180-degree turn pivots about the camera's own up axis.

The camera's position does not change. APoint becomes the new OrbitCenter and Distance becomes the camera's distance from it, so later orbiting circles that point. If the camera already stands on APoint, within 1E-6, nothing happens.

Parameters

The point to face, in the rig parent's space.

Public procedure SetOrientation(const Q: TQuat);

Sets the orientation directly, in the rig parent's space.

Q is normalised, then split into Azimuth, PolarAngle and Roll. Of the two angle sets that give the same rotation, the one nearest the current angles is chosen, so the values change continuously and PolarAngle can run past +/-90. At an exact pole Azimuth is held and the rest of the twist goes into Roll. Azimuth is wrapped into 0 to 360; PolarAngle and Roll are not wrapped.

OrbitCenter and Distance do not change, so the camera swings round the centre.

Parameters
Q
The new orientation. A quaternion of near-zero length is treated as the identity.

Public procedure Orbit(const DeltaAzimuth, DeltaPolar: Single);

Adds to Azimuth and PolarAngle, for example from a mouse drag.

Azimuth is wrapped into 0 to 360. PolarAngle is not limited, so the camera can carry on over a pole. Roll, Distance and OrbitCenter do not change.

Public procedure Zoom(const Factor: Single);

Multiplies Distance by Factor, for example from the mouse wheel.

A Factor below 1 moves the camera closer. A Factor of zero or less is ignored, and Distance never goes below 0.001. In orthographic mode the apparent size depends on OrthoHeight, not on Distance, so zooming this way has no visible effect there.

Parameters
Factor
The multiplier; must be greater than zero to have any effect.

Public function FitToBounds(const ABox: TBoundingBox; const AMargin: Single = 0): Boolean; overload;

Makes ABox as large as possible in the viewport for the current angles.

The fit is for the camera's current Azimuth, PolarAngle and Roll, which are never changed. OrbitCenter moves and Distance changes; in orthographic or isometric mode OrthoHeight, and OrthoWidth if it is non-zero, are scaled as well. The view is centred on the box's eight corners, and they touch both edges of the tighter axis. The frustum, including the aspect ratio, comes from GetProjectionMatrix for the viewport's size.

Changing an angle afterwards can push the box out of view, so call FitToBounds again after every change, or use FitToBoundsMax. In perspective mode every corner is also kept at least 1 unit in front of the camera, FMX's near plane, which can make a very deep box smaller than the view.

Parameters
ABox
The box to frame, in the rig parent's space. For a box in an object's own local space, use the overload that takes ASpace.
AMargin
Padding as a fraction of the view: 0.1 makes the box 1/1.1 of the view on the tighter axis. Negative values count as 0.
Returns

True if the box was framed; False, with nothing changed, if the box is empty, there is no viewport, or it has zero width or height.

Public function FitToBoundsMax(const ABox: TBoundingBox; const AMargin: Single = 0): Boolean; overload;

Makes ABox as large as possible while it stays visible at every angle.

Azimuth, PolarAngle and Roll together reach every orientation, so the box can end up in any rotation about the orbit centre. All those rotations together fill the sphere around the box's centre that reaches its farthest corner, so that sphere is what is fitted: it is the smallest shape that is safe for every angle. OrbitCenter moves to the box's centre and Distance, or the orthographic size, is set; the angles are not changed and can then be changed freely without refitting. The overloads that take a model or ASpace centre the sphere on that object's centre of rotation instead.

The box looks smaller than with FitToBounds, by up to the ratio of the box's half diagonal to its smaller visible half-size. In perspective mode the sphere is kept at least 1 unit in front of the camera, FMX's near plane.

Parameters
ABox
The box to frame, in the rig parent's space. For a box in an object's own local space, use the overload that takes ASpace.
AMargin
Padding as a fraction of the view: 0.1 makes the sphere 1/1.1 of the view on the tighter axis. Negative values count as 0.
Returns

True if the box was framed; False, with nothing changed, if the box is empty, there is no viewport, or it has zero width or height.

Makes a Gorilla model's bounding box as large as possible while it stays visible at every angle.

Takes AModel.GetAnimatedBoundingBox, the current pose's box in the model's local space, and passes it with AModel as the space to the overload that takes ASpace, so the model's transform is applied once. Public function FitToBoundsMax(const ABox: TBoundingBox; const ASpace: TControl3D; const AMargin: Single = 0): Boolean; overload;

Fits a box given in ASpace's local coordinates for every angle.

All eight corners are carried through ASpace's position, rotation and scale into the rig parent's space. The sphere is centred on ASpace's centre of rotation, its RotationCenter carried into the same space, and reaches the farthest transformed corner from it, so non-uniform scale is handled. The centre of rotation can lie outside the box; the camera then circles it, which keeps it steady while an animation moves the box. With ASpace nil the sphere is centred on the box. It is then fitted as in the other overloads, using the viewport's size.

AMargin
Padding as a fraction of the view: 0.1 makes the sphere 1/1.1 of the view on the tighter axis.
Returns

True if the box was framed; False, with nothing changed, if the box is empty, there is no viewport, or it has zero width or height.

Public property Rig: TDummy read FAzimuthPivot;

The root of the pivot chain; set Rig.Parent to place the camera in a scene.

The same object as AzimuthPivot. Its Position is OrbitCenter. Freeing Rig frees the whole chain, the camera included.

Public property AzimuthPivot: TDummy read FAzimuthPivot;

The pivot that turns about Y for Azimuth; the root of the chain, same as Rig.

Its rotation is overwritten whenever an angle changes, so do not set it directly.

Public property PolarPivot: TDummy read FPolarPivot;

The pivot that turns about X for PolarAngle; a child of AzimuthPivot.

Its rotation is overwritten whenever an angle changes, so do not set it directly.

Public property RollPivot: TDummy read FRollPivot;

The pivot that turns about Z for Roll; a child of PolarPivot and the camera's parent.

Its rotation is overwritten whenever an angle changes, so do not set it directly.

Public property OrbitCenter: TPosition3D read GetOrbitCenter;

The point the camera orbits and looks at, in the rig parent's space.

This is Rig's Position object, so writing to it, for example OrbitCenter.Point := P, moves the centre and the camera with it. It is named so as not to clash with TCamera.Target, which should be left nil on this camera.

Public property Orientation: TQuat read GetOrientation write SetOrientation;

The camera's orientation as a quaternion in the rig parent's space.

Reading builds Qy(Azimuth) * Qx(-PolarAngle) * Qz(Roll). Writing calls SetOrientation, which splits the quaternion back into the three angles.

See also
SetOrientation
Sets the orientation directly, in the rig parent's space.
Published property Azimuth: Single read FAzimuth write SetAzimuth;

Rotation about the vertical Y axis through the orbit centre, in degrees.

Written values are wrapped into 0 to 360. The pivots update at once.

Published property PolarAngle: Single read FPolarAngle write SetPolarAngle;

Elevation of the camera above the orbit centre, in degrees.

0 is level with the centre, +90 is directly above looking down and -90 directly below looking up. Not limited: values past +/-90 carry the camera over the pole and down the far side, upside down.

Published property Roll: Single read FRoll write SetRoll;

Rotation of the camera about its own view direction, in degrees.

Neither wrapped nor limited. It never changes where the camera points.

Published property Distance: Single read FDistance write SetDistance;

Distance from the orbit centre to the camera, in scene units.

Defaults to 10 and is clamped to at least 0.001. In orthographic mode it does not change the apparent size; FitToBounds still sets it to keep the near plane clear of the framed object.

Published property Locked: Boolean read FLocked write FLocked;

While True, nothing done through this class moves or turns the camera.

Setting Azimuth, PolarAngle, Roll, Distance or Orientation, and calling LookAt, Orbit, Zoom, MoveCenterTo or RestoreFraming, then do nothing, and every FitToBounds and FitToBoundsMax returns False without changing anything. Reading is unaffected, so SaveFraming still works. It does not stop code writing straight to the inherited properties, such as ProjectionMode and OrthoHeight, or to OrbitCenter's position; callers that honour the lock must check it before they do. False by default.
