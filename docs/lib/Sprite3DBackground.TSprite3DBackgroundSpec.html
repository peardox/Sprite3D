Sprite3DBackground

type TSprite3DBackgroundSpec = record

Description

Describes a background: its kind, colours and checker size.

A plain record, copied by value. Default gives the application's starting background: None, with a checker of 64-pixel squares in two greys ready for Transparent. Colours are used opaque; their alpha is ignored.

Public class function Default: TSprite3DBackgroundSpec; static;

The starting background: None.

The checker has 64-pixel squares of light and mid grey, Colour is mid grey and the gradient runs from light grey at the top to dark grey at the bottom, ready for when another kind is chosen.

Public procedure GradientAxis(const AWidth, AHeight: Single; out ADX, ADY, ALength: Single);

The direction the gradient runs in an area of the given size, and its length.

The direction is a unit vector with X to the right and Y down; the length is the area's extent along it, from the starting edge or corner to the opposite one.

Parameters
AWidth
The area's width, in any unit.
AHeight
The area's height, in the same unit.
ADX
The direction's X component.
ADY
The direction's Y component; never negative.
ALength
The gradient's length, in the unit of AWidth and AHeight.
