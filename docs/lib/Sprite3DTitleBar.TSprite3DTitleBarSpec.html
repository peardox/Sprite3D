Fields

Public UseSystem: Boolean;

True to leave title bars as the system draws them; the colours are then ignored.

Public Caption: TAlphaColor;

The caption's background colour; its brightness also picks dark or light where colours are not possible.

Public Text: TAlphaColor;

The caption text's colour.

Public Border: TAlphaColor;

The window border's colour, or TAlphaColors.Null for the system's.
