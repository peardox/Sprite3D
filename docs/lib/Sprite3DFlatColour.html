Unit Sprite3DFlatColour

Description
Functions and Procedures

Description

Flat colours and simple gradients for Gorilla3D materials without a shader, for Metal.

On macOS and iOS Gorilla3D draws through Metal, where a material's SurfaceShader is not supported and a plain material colour is not reliably drawn. These routines colour a TGorillaDefaultMaterialSource through a small texture instead: one colour, or a blend from the top of the texture to the bottom.

Texturing must be switched on before a texture is given and before the material is first drawn, or Gorilla reports "Texture0 not activated in material". So call PrepareFlatMaterial once, as the material is made and before any object uses it; SetFlatColour and SetFlatGradient then only change the texture's pixels.

The unit compiles on every platform; the rest of the project uses it only where Metal is in use. For the main thread only.

procedure PrepareFlatMaterial(const AMaterial: TGorillaDefaultMaterialSource);

Sets a new material up to take its colour from a texture.

Unlit, without specular light, with texturing on, a white Diffuse colour so the texture's colours are left as they are, and a black texture to begin with. Call it before the material is given to any object.

Parameters
AMaterial
The material, newly made.

procedure SetFlatGradient(const AMaterial: TGorillaDefaultMaterialSource; const ATop, ABottom: TAlphaColor);

Colours a material prepared by PrepareFlatMaterial with a blend from ATop to ABottom.

The blend runs down the texture, from its first row to its last, in 256 steps. Alpha is ignored.

Parameters
AMaterial
The material.
ATop
The colour at the top of the texture.
ABottom
The colour at the bottom of the texture.
