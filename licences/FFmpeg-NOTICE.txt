FFmpeg libraries
================

Sprite3D's MP4 video export uses the FFmpeg libraries avutil, avcodec,
avformat and, where present, swscale, version 8.1, when they are installed
beside it. They are not part of Sprite3D itself.

FFmpeg is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1
or later (LGPL v2.1+). The text of the LGPL v2.1 is reproduced in this
program under "GNU LGPL v2.1". The libraries shipped with Sprite3D are LGPL
builds of FFmpeg, configured without --enable-gpl and --enable-nonfree.

Sprite3D loads the libraries at run time and is not linked against them. You
may replace them with your own builds of FFmpeg 8.1 with the same library
names.

The FFmpeg source code for this version is available from:

  https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-8.1.tar.xz
  https://git.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.git, tag n8.1
